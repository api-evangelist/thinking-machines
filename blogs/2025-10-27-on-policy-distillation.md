LLMs are capable of expert performance in focused domains, a result of several capabilities stacked together: perception of input, knowledge retrieval, plan selection, and reliable execution. This requires a stack of training approaches, which we can divide into three broad stages: Pre-training teaches general capacities such as language use, broad reasoning, and world knowledge. Mid-training imparts domain knowledge, such as code, medical databases, or internal company documents.
